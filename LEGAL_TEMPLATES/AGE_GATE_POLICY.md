# Age Gate & Minor Protection Policy

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

## 1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Rain is **an 18+ only service.** We take child safety extremely seriously.

This policy explains:
- How we enforce the 18+ restriction
- What we do if we discover a minor using the service
- How we prevent predatory behavior
- Our zero-tolerance policy for CSAM

---

## 2. Age Verification Methods

### 2.1 Multi-Layer Verification

We enforce age restrictions through:

1. **Age Gate Checkbox**
   - Users must acknowledge they are 18+
   - They must accept our Terms of Service
   - Checkbox cannot be bypassed
   - Required for signup and guest join

2. **CAPTCHA Verification (hCaptcha)**
   - Prevents bot accounts from bypassing age gate
   - Requires human interaction to prove awareness
   - Flagged as suspicious if failed multiple times

3. **Device Fingerprinting**
   - Tracks IP address + browser fingerprint
   - Detects if same device creates multiple accounts (evasion)
   - Flags high-risk patterns (new device, suspicious location)
   - Can block devices with history of violations

4. **Behavioral Analysis**
   - Monitors for patterns consistent with minors:
     - Immature username or bio
     - Content focused on school, homework, youth culture
     - Interaction with other flagged accounts
     - Age-inappropriate behavior in conversations
   - Flags for manual review

### 2.2 User Reporting

Any user can report suspected minors:
1. Click "Report" on their profile
2. Select reason: "Minor Safety"
3. Add details about why you think they're underage
4. Report is escalated to moderation team immediately

## 3. What We Do If We Find A Minor

### 3.1 Immediate Action (Within 1 Hour)
1. **Account Termination** — Account is immediately suspended
2. **Data Deletion** — All profile, messages, and device data deleted
3. **Device Ban** — Device fingerprint blacklisted; cannot create new accounts on that device
4. **Session Revocation** — All active sessions ended
5. **Log Creation** — Incident documented in audit log with timestamp

### 3.2 Investigation (Within 24 Hours)
1. **Pattern Analysis** — Did they match with adults? What content was exchanged?
2. **Risk Assessment** — Were they exposed to sexual content or predatory behavior?
3. **Evidence Collection** — Screenshots, metadata, interaction logs
4. **Contact Info** — Attempt to collect information about the minor (for safety notifications)

### 3.3 Reporting to Authorities (Within 72 Hours)

If the minor was exposed to sexual content or predatory behavior:

1. **CyberTipline Report** (NCMEC)
   - We file a report with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children
   - URL: https://cybertipline.org
   - Includes date, time, minor's age, content exchanged
   - Provides contact info if available

2. **Local Law Enforcement**
   - We contact the local police in the jurisdiction where the minor is located
   - Provide all evidence and context
   - Full cooperation with investigation

3. **Parent/Guardian Notification** (if contact info available)
   - Inform them their minor used an 18+ service
   - Explain what content they may have been exposed to
   - Provide resources for support

## 4. Predator Detection & Prevention

### 4.1 What Constitutes Predatory Behavior

Predatory behavior includes (non-exhaustive):
- Asking a minor to send sexual or intimate images
- Attempting to arrange in-person meetings with sexual intent
- Grooming: slowly building trust to isolate and exploit
- Sextortion: blackmailing with intimate images
- Soliciting sexual conversation from minors

### 4.2 How We Detect It

1. **Keyword Scanning** — Messages are scanned for grooming language:
   - "How old are you?"
   - "Do you have pics?"
   - "Don't tell anyone"
   - "We're special / secret"
   - [Full keyword list in moderation database]

2. **Pattern Detection**
   - Adults matching with multiple suspected minors
   - Repeated requests for images or personal info
   - Attempts to move conversation off-platform ("text me at...")
   - Progressive escalation of sexual content

3. **User Reports**
   - Any user can report predatory behavior
   - Reports are prioritized and reviewed within 1 hour
   - High-severity reports escalated immediately

### 4.3 Zero-Tolerance Enforcement

If we detect predatory behavior:
1. **Immediate Ban** — Account is terminated permanently
2. **Device Ban** — Device is blacklisted
3. **IP Ban** — IP address flagged (may block)
4. **Law Enforcement Referral** — We report to FBI/local police
5. **CyberTipline Report** — If CSAM is involved
6. **Evidence Preservation** — All logs and messages archived for law enforcement

**We do not give second chances for predatory behavior.**

## 5. CSAM (Child Sexual Abuse Material) — CRITICAL

### 5.1 Definition
CSAM includes any sexual or exploitative content depicting minors (under 18), whether:
- Photos or videos
- AI-generated or real
- Suggestive or explicit
- Drawn or animated

### 5.2 Zero Tolerance
If CSAM is found:
1. **Immediate Deletion** — All copies deleted within 1 minute
2. **Account Termination** — User permanently banned, all data deleted
3. **Device Ban** — Device blacklisted globally
4. **Law Enforcement Report** — Within 1 hour to FBI and local police
5. **NCMEC Report** — To CyberTipline within 1 hour
6. **Evidence Preservation** — Hash stored in law enforcement database

### 5.3 Reporting CSAM
If you discover CSAM on Rain:

## 8. Third-Party & Legal Obligations

### 8.1 Mandatory Reporting
As a platform, we are required by law (18 U.S.C. § 2258) to report CSAM to:
- National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
- FBI & local law enforcement

We take this obligation seriously and report within 1 hour of discovery.

### 8.2 Law Enforcement Cooperation
We maintain a process for:
- Processing valid law enforcement requests (search warrants, subpoenas)
- Preserving evidence of abuse
- Providing data exports and transaction logs
- Appearing as a witness in court if needed

### 8.3 Compliance with Regulations

We comply with:
- **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) — though Rain is 18+ only
- **PROTECT Act** — No CSAM distribution, knowingly facilitated
- **CyberTipline Requirements** — Mandatory reporting
- **State laws** — Age verification and safety standards
- **GDPR & CCPA** — Privacy and data protection (especially for minors if discovered)
